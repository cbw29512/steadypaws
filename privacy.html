Privacy

Their care notes belong with you.

Your Pet’s Health Log does not require an account, email address, or health profile to use the care paperwork.

Names and photos stay on your device

If you choose to personalize a PDF, the name and photo are processed in your browser and placed into the downloaded copy on your device. Your Pet’s Health Log does not send the selected photo or entered name to a Your Pet’s Health Log server.

Quick Phone Log records stay on your device

The Quick Phone Log stores the pet name and type, optional veterinarian details, and saved check-ins in this browser's local storage. Photos attached to check-ins are stored in this browser's local database. Those records are not submitted to Your Pet’s Health Log. Removing a pet from the Quick Phone Log removes that pet's saved check-ins and photos from this device; clearing this site's browser storage can also remove them. Anyone who can use the same browser profile may be able to see locally saved records.

Worksheet entries are saved on your device

The accessible web worksheets auto-save what you type to your browser's local storage so your notes can survive a reload and remain available offline. Those entries are not submitted to Your Pet’s Health Log. Anyone who can use the same browser profile may be able to see locally saved entries. Each worksheet includes a Clear saved form data button, and clearing this site's browser storage can also remove saved entries.

Basic website requests

Like most websites, the hosting provider may process ordinary technical request information needed to deliver the site, such as an IP address, browser information, requested page, and timestamps. Your Pet’s Health Log does not add an advertising tracker or health-data analytics script to the site.

External support links

If you choose the Buy Me a Coffee support link, you leave Your Pet’s Health Log and that service applies its own privacy practices.
