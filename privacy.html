Privacy

Their care notes belong with you.

Steady Paws does not require an account, email address, or health profile to use the care paperwork.

Names and photos stay on your device

If you choose to personalize a PDF, the name and photo are processed in your browser and placed into the downloaded copy on your device. Steady Paws does not send the selected photo or entered name to a Steady Paws server.

The worksheets do not submit your entries

The accessible web worksheets are local form controls for organizing and printing your notes. They do not have a submit button and do not send what you type to Steady Paws.

Basic website requests

Like most websites, the hosting provider may process ordinary technical request information needed to deliver the site, such as an IP address, browser information, requested page, and timestamps. Steady Paws does not add an advertising tracker or health-data analytics script to the site.

External support links

If you choose the Buy Me a Coffee support link, you leave Steady Paws and that service applies its own privacy practices.
