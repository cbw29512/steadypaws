Built with care

The form should fit the family member — not the other way around.

A rabbit is not a small dog. A bird is not a cat with feathers. A reptile, horse, fish, or guinea pig has different things worth noticing. Steady Paws keeps those differences in the forms while keeping the experience simple for the person doing the caring.

Personal: the site starts with who you love, not a medical catalog.

Specific: observation fields change when the species and health problem change.

Free: every tracker can be downloaded without an account or email address.

One important thing

You know them. Their veterinary team knows the medicine.

Steady Paws helps you organize what you notice between visits. It does not diagnose disease, recommend medication doses, or set medical or husbandry targets. Use the plan given by a veterinarian experienced with their species, and seek prompt veterinary care for urgent or concerning changes.
