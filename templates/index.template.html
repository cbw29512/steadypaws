Broad coverage without pretending

Many companion animals. Species-specific where it matters.

A rabbit is not a small dog, and a reptile is not a furry pet with scales. Steady Paws separates monitoring fields when physiology, husbandry, and common chronic problems differ.

Small mammals: rabbit, guinea pig, ferret, chinchilla, hamster, rat and mouse resources.

Exotics: bird, reptile, fish and amphibian logs include habitat or husbandry observations where relevant.

Equids: horse trackers include hoof, body-condition, respiratory and metabolic observations.

Important

Your veterinary team makes the medical decisions.

Steady Paws provides organizational tools only. It does not diagnose disease, recommend medication doses, or establish species-specific temperature, humidity, water-quality, diet, glucose, blood-pressure, or other medical targets. Use targets supplied by a veterinarian or other qualified animal-health professional, and seek prompt veterinary care for urgent or concerning changes.
