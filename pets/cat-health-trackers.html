Care & safety

Your notes help tell the story. Your veterinarian makes the medical decisions.

Steady Paws does not diagnose disease, recommend medication doses, or set treatment targets. Follow the plan from a veterinarian experienced with cats and seek prompt veterinary care for urgent or concerning changes.
