Built to grow

Dogs and cats first - without pretending that means every pet.

This first comprehensive library focuses on common long-term monitoring needs in dogs and cats. Future species can get their own properly researched tools rather than recycled dog-and-cat forms.

Free: no account or email wall.

Private: the site does not collect your pet's health logs.

Printable: every tracker is designed for ordinary Letter-size printing.

Veterinary-centered: the sheets organize observations; they do not diagnose or prescribe.

Important

Your veterinarian makes the medical decisions.

Steady Paws provides organizational tools, not diagnosis or treatment. Do not start, stop, or change medication or dosing because of a tracker. For urgent or concerning symptoms - especially breathing difficulty, prolonged or repeated seizures, collapse, or sudden severe changes - contact a veterinarian or emergency veterinary service.
