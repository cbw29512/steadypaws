Why Steady Paws

Useful before it is complicated.

The goal is not to replace veterinary advice. It is to help you capture what happened between appointments so your veterinarian has better information to work with.

Practical: every tracker is designed to print cleanly on standard Letter paper.

Private: the current site does not require an account or collect health logs.

Focused: no diagnosis engine and no medication-dose recommendations.

Important

Your veterinarian is the medical decision-maker.

Steady Paws provides organizational and educational tools, not diagnosis or treatment. Follow your veterinary team's instructions, and contact a veterinarian or emergency veterinary service for urgent or concerning symptoms.
